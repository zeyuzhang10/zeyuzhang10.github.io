About Me

I am Zeyu Zhang (张泽渝). My research interests center on computer vision, cross-modal retrieval, and multimodal pre-training, with recent work spanning vehicle re-identification and medical image analysis.

My academic path includes the School of Mathematics and Computer Science at Nanchang University and, beginning in September 2026, the University of Science and Technology of China.
